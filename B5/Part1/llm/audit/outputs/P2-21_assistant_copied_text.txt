## 3. Raw evidence preservation rules

Keep the 20 original prompts and 20 raw LLM outputs unchanged. Save each from the relevant conversation stage without correcting wording, changing provisional classifications or replacing early conclusions with the final nine-instance inventory.

In particular, preserve the original geocoding pattern suggestions, the provisional timeline–map Observer, the earlier uncertain Turbo classification and the two candidates discovered during the repository sweep. Their subsequent rejection, acceptance or revision is part of the audit evidence.

The `verification_file` column identifies later source-based review outputs that support or qualify earlier conclusions. These later LLM reviews are not substitutes for independent human sign-off. Where a corresponding source-check document or reviewer record is prepared, preserve it separately, with its reviewer, date and evidence; update references only to files that have actually been saved.

Also keep the final frozen `patterns.csv`, `pattern_counts.csv`, Mermaid sources and verification records separate from the raw LLM outputs. Do not overwrite stage 18 or 19 raw responses with corrected or exported artifacts.
