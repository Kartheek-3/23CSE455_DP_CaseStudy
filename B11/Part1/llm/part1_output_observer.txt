What the code actually does (verified):

- These call EmailServiceImpl (hearingReminder / sendMailWithAttachement /
  BirthdayReminder) which emails users.

Why UNCERTAIN rather than Confirmed:
A true Observer has a Subject that maintains a list of registered Observers and
notifies each on a state change. Here there is NO such registration - a timer
(Spring's scheduler) fires on a cron/fixed-rate schedule and the scheduled method
directly invokes the email service. There is no subscribe/unsubscribe, no observer
collection, no subject-observer decoupling. It is scheduler-triggered broadcast, not
subject-observer notification.

Recommendation: record as an uncertain Observer candidate with this reasoning. CH02
(adding an SMS channel via a CaseRegister that holds registered observers) is what
turns this into a genuine Observer - that evolution is the honest way to claim the
pattern.
