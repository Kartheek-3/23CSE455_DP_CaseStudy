    Source traces establish Template Method and the introduced Strategy.
    Part 3 outputs establish behavior of the eight isolated examples.
    Fresh Rails veriﬁcation: 36 examples, zero failures; all 12 migrations applied. Earlier
    HTTP startup receipt is retained separately.
    Historical Prompt 02/03 wording and some model/date metadata were not retained; gaps
    are disclosed.

Finding: use a pattern when the collaboration and design pressure justify it. A smaller
evidence-backed inventory is easier to defend.
