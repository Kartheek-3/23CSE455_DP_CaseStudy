# Part 1 prompt provenance

`Part1_prompt.txt` is the current Part 1 discovery specification used to organize the scoped P1 audit and its handoff artifacts. The historical Gemini interaction used a shorter prompt, preserved at `Part1/llm/historical_discovery_prompt.txt`; its saved response remains `Part1/llm/raw_output.txt`. The historical row in `Part1/llm.csv` and canonical `data/llm.csv` points to that historical prompt.

`Part1_prompt_Sai_Samay.txt` is a reconstructed, role-specific assignment brief for Sai Samay's shared P1 discovery contribution. It is a task specification, not a record of a historical prompt or model run.

The scoped source audit and `patterns_p1_discovery.csv` are later audit artifacts. They are recorded separately from the historical model output. The P1 prompt describes a discovery package; it does not make the audit exhaustive beyond the listed inspected paths.
