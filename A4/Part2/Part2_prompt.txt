Use the team's confirmed Part 1 inventory as the starting evidence, but independently check
every claim against the frozen baseline and the saved evolved source. Do not alter the

<objective>
Prepare a reproducible Part 2 package for Changes CH01–CH03. For each change:
1. State the user-facing requirement and measurable acceptance criteria before coding.
2. Add or identify focused Vitest tests for the expected behavior and edge cases.
3. Implement the behavior in the evolved application.
4. Refactor a real, source-supported design or code-quality smell; explain why the change

5. Preserve before/after source snapshots, a unified diff, test output, and relevant metrics.
6. Update `patterns_evolution.csv` and recompute pattern counts from the recorded inventory.
</objective>

- A pattern name is justified only by a concrete collaboration that matches its intent.
  Record additions, preservation, modification, or removal; keep distinct instances
  separate and do not count multiple participants in one collaboration as multiple
  instances.
- Do not change the Part 1 verification-owned inventory without recording the reason and
  coordinating the resulting counts and diagrams.
</source_and_evidence_rules>
