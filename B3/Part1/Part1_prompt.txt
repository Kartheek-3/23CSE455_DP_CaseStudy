
Part 1 — Existing-System Investigation and Design-Pattern Identification

Repository:
https://github.com/manjurulhoque/django-job-portal

Recorded baseline commit:
f6e5efb33e9aac7334a364ee46d6e2ad1321db30

Objective:
Analyze the existing Django Job Portal using actual source-code evidence. Identify confirmed design-pattern collaborations, document uncertain candidates, reconstruct the architecture, and prepare the Part 1 inventory.

Tasks:
1. Inspect the README, dependency files, settings, models, serializers, views, permissions, URLs, GraphQL components, and relevant tests.
2. Identify the application's implemented domain concepts and trace representative request flows where supported by the code.
3. Reconstruct the architecture and describe the responsibilities and relationships of major components.
4. Investigate GoF and framework-related patterns, including Strategy, Template Method, Factory Method, Observer, State, Adapter, Decorator, and other plausible candidates.
5. For every candidate, document its pattern name, participants, source locations, supporting evidence, confidence, benefits, limitations, and any additional verification required.
6. Do not classify a pattern merely because a class uses inheritance, a framework hook, a callback, or a familiar name. Mark incomplete evidence as partial or uncertain.
7. Create:
   - Part1/patterns.csv
   - Part1/Pattern_count.csv
   - Part1/pattern_architecture diagram source/image
   - Part1/Part1_prompt.txt
   - Part1/llm.csv

Required patterns.csv columns:
source_version,pattern_name,pattern_type,instance_name,participant_roles,source_locations,intent_and_evidence

Required Pattern_count.csv columns:
source_version,summary,pattern_name,count

Required llm.csv columns:
part,model_and_date,prompt_file,input_file,output_file,evaluation,verification_file

Baseline inventory recorded in the case study:
- Strategy: 2 instances
- Template Method: 2 instances
- Factory Method: 1 qualified candidate

This is a total of five documented instances across three pattern types. Preserve the qualification on Factory Method and do not treat uncertain candidates as confirmed patterns.

Quality requirements:
- Ground classifications in source code and cite file paths and line ranges.
- Count distinct collaborations consistently.
- Keep baseline findings separate from later Part 2 changes.
- Record actual test commands and results; do not invent successful tests, metrics, or verification.
- If exact historical prompts or raw outputs were not retained, disclose that limitation in llm.csv.
- Ensure the report, diagrams, and CSV counts agree.
